Terms of Service — TikTok AutoPoster

1. What this app does

TikTok AutoPoster is a personal Android app. It takes video files from a Google Drive folder you choose and uploads them to your own TikTok accounts using TikTok's official Content Posting API, on a schedule you set. It can manage up to three TikTok accounts, never uploads the same video twice to the same account, and adds an automatic title with hashtags to each upload.

2. Your accounts and access

You log in with TikTok's official Login Kit. Your login happens inside TikTok itself — this app never sees or stores your TikTok password. The app stores only the official access tokens TikTok issues, encrypted on your own device.

  • Automatic titles and hashtags are generated from file names; review them — you remain responsible for what is published.

4. How posting works

Posting uses TikTok's official API only. Public (direct) posting requires TikTok's Direct Post approval for the connected developer app; until that approval is granted, uploads are sent to your TikTok drafts and you publish them manually inside the TikTok app. Upload frequency and timing are fully controlled by you in the app's settings.
