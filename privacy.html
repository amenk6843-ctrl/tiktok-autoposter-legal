Privacy Policy — TikTok AutoPoster

1. What data the app handles

TikTok AutoPoster stores the following ONLY on your own Android device, in encrypted storage:

  • TikTok login tokens (access token and refresh token) issued via TikTok Login Kit
  • Your TikTok account identifiers (open ID, display name) for the accounts you connect
  • The TikTok developer Client Key and Client Secret you enter
  • The Google Drive API key and Drive folder link you enter
  • A local list of already-uploaded video IDs (to prevent duplicate uploads) and an activity log

2. What is NOT collected

  • Your TikTok password — login happens inside TikTok itself and the password never touches this app.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no location data.
  • Nothing is sent to the app operator or any third party besides the services below.

3. How data is used and shared

Stored data is used solely to do what you asked: list videos from your Drive folder and upload them to your TikTok accounts on your schedule. Data leaves your device only to TikTok's official API and Google's Drive API, as required to perform those functions.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it: disconnect an account in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app — all stored tokens, keys, and logs are deleted.

5. Security

Tokens and keys are kept in Android's encrypted storage (backed by the device keystore). No method is perfectly secure, but data never leaves your device except to TikTok and Google as described above.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and should only be used with TikTok accounts that meet TikTok's age requirements.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always the one published at this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the app operator through the contact details listed on the app's page in the TikTok developer portal.

Effective: October 2, 2026.
